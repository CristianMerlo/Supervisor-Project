Introduction:
The “General guideline for the spare parts servicing at MPCS KG” does not replace our “General 
Terms and Conditions” but provides the basis for a smooth and efficient process for supplying 
spare parts.
The spare parts service is an important service for our customers. Our objective is to ensure a 
smooth delivery of the ordered spare parts. To achieve this, we require your help. By complying 
with the following points, it can be ensured that Melitta Professional Coffee Solutions GmbH & 
Co. KG will be able to make optimum deliveries to its customers.
Please note that orders are binding and must be made in writing. Delivery times and dates apply 
in all cases as ex-works from Minden.

1. Ensuring the supply of spare parts
The spare parts service at MPCS KG ensures the supply of the respective spare parts (E) and 
wearing parts (V) for 10 years after the purchase of a coffee machine. This declaration is an 
internal agreement and can be altered anytime by observing an appropriate interim period. Parts 
that are not labeled as such explicitly as well as part requirements beyond the stated period 
must be requested specifically from the spare parts service.
2. New part orders
• New parts are marked with an “E” in the column “Code” in the spare parts catalogs.
• On your order please state your customer number, your order number and your desired ship-
ping method.
• Both the material number and the material text must be listed. This helps to prevent delivery 
mistakes as the material number and material text can be cross-referenced.
• Please write handwritten orders legibly in block capitals.
• Important:  Replacement parts and warranty parts must not be listed on new parts 
orders!4 / 52

3. Warranty claims
• In general Melitta Professional Coffee Solutions GmbH & Co. KG provides a 12-month 
warranty on new parts and a 6-month warranty on replacement parts.
• However, the warranty can only be granted if the returned parts are provided with a fully 
completed repair label.
• Repair labels can be obtained free-of-charge using the following material number from the 
spare parts service of Melitta Professional Coffee Solutions  GmbH & Co. KG:
German Mat. no. 2950202
English Mat. no. 2950550
French Mat. no. 2950551
• If a warranty replacement is required for machine parts, then the corresponding parts are to 
be listed separately in the warranty order.
• Any stickers (3 or 4 digits) affixed to parts must not be removed, otherwise the warranty 
claim is void.
• On the first item line of the order, please write “Warranty Order” as the title.
• Important: Only warranty parts must be listed on warranty parts orders!
• Warranty claims are clarified upon receipt of the parts return delivery. For justified claims a 
replacement will be delivered.
• The findings of the assessment will be communicated to the customer.
• If claims are not justified, the customer must place an order for new parts.
• Advance deliveries of parts under warranty may be obtained only in very urgent cases, after 
consultation with our spare parts service. These parts will be charged and – if the warranty 
claim is justified – they will be credited later after receipt of the parts at Melitta Professional 
Coffee Solutions GmbH & Co. KG.
• For wearing parts (marked with a “V” in the spare parts documentation) no warranty can be 
granted.
4. Replacement parts
• Replacement parts are items which can be inspected and repaired and restored to 100% 
working order by Melitta Professional Coffee Solutions GmbH & Co. KG and then reused.
• Replacement parts are marked with an “R” in the column “Code” in the spare parts docu-
mentation.
• Orders for replacement parts may only contain the same number of parts as those sent back 
for inspection.
• Electronic assembly groups without any attached error description on the repair label cannot 
be inspected and can therefore only be invoiced as a new part.5 / 52

• If a repair is no longer possible for a delivered part, there is no entitlement to the delivery of 
a replacement part. A new part will be delivered as a replacement and invoiced accordingly.
• We ask that you clean the outside of the replacement parts before sending them to us. For 
items not cleaned Melitta Professional Coffee Solutions GmbH & Co. KG will invoice you for 
the cost of cleaning.
• On the first item line of the order, please state “Replacement Parts” as the title.
• Important: Replacement items have their own material numbers! New parts and 
warranty parts must not be listed on replacement parts orders!
5. Wearing Parts
• Wearing parts are marked with a “V” in the column “Code” in the spare parts documentation.
• A warranty can not be granted on these parts. They can only be supplied as a new part.
6. Unmarked items or items on request
• Unmarked items or “items on request” (marked with an “A” in the column “Code”) are neither 
spare parts nor wearing parts.
• These parts are generally not always available ex warehouse. Please ask for prices and 
delivery dates individually. Under certain circumstances it may not be possible to obtain and 
deliver an item.
7. Spare parts documentation
• Our spare parts documentation is updated internally by our documentation department.
• The spare parts documentation for our products can be viewed at any time with all the latest 
changes in the download areas.
8. General
• For organizational reasons, we ask that, if possible, you place only one order per week 
(collective order).
• If you do not specify any freight type to us (e.g. “for collection”, “Express”, etc.), we will 
select the cheapest method from our perspective for you.
• Any shipping complaints have to be addressed to Melitta Professional Coffee Solutions 
GmbH & Co. KG within 10 days of receipt of the goods.
• Each return shipment must have attached a delivery note which states what to do with the 
returned item.
• Please pack up the items carefully (risk of transport damage)! Parts received at Melitta 
Professional Coffee Solutions GmbH & Co. KG which are damaged due to insufficient pack-
aging will be invoiced to the customer!6 / 52
